# Project Handoff

Last updated: 2026-05-29

## Current State

The `outlier_migrate` COLM workshop paper is submission-ready as a scoped
mechanism/regime paper. It is not an ICLR-ready positive-method paper.

Current paper story:

- Protected channel identities drift substantially during long reasoning.
- Static, hard-switch, prediction, residual, and surface/channel-set remedies
  failed or remained ambiguous under matched controls.
- ParoQuant-style rotation is the strongest baseline/remedy class, but it is
  not our method.
- The contribution is the drift measurement, failure taxonomy, regime guidance,
  and systems cost envelope for future residual-correction designs.

Do not reopen method search for this submission cycle unless a new owner writes
a fresh preregistration and accepts the scope risk.

## If Work Resumes Later

The live positive-method gap remains: no new method beyond the ParoQuant-style
baseline survived the gates. For a future ICLR-scale project, the next owner
should not start from this paper-writing branch. They should start from a new
gated research plan with:

- frozen train/confirmation splits;
- strict same-family and cross-family falsification pairs;
- official strong baselines where possible;
- paired uncertainty and multiple-testing control;
- a clear target for a positive method that beats or robustifies rotation.
