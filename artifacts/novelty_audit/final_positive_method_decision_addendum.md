The final gate avoids a weak novelty claim. No DriftRot-style method beat or robustified ParoQuant under the bounded gates.

Safe claims:

- Long-decode channel-set drift measurement across architectures.
- Failure taxonomy for channel-set methods, hard switches, predictors, WJAC, K-RES residual-energy proxy, and M-SURFACE internals.
- Regime/protocol guidance that treats ParoQuant as a strong prior-work baseline.
- Residual-correction systems model as design analysis only, not a successful method.

- ParoQuant is our method.
- K-RES is a positive method.
